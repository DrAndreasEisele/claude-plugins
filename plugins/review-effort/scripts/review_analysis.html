What the numbers mean

Per day

Reading Effort Estimation ( words/min) Thinking & other Waiting (Claude working)

Reading Effort Estimation is the reading time Claude's answers ask for: the prose words of all answers, divided by words per minute. Counted like the review-effort line: code and URLs are left out.

Waiting runs from your prompt to Claude's last message for that request. It includes time Claude waits for your approval.

Thinking & other is the measured time from Claude's last message to your next prompt, minus the reading time, capped at minutes per request. Time spent on something else counts for at most that long, so heavy multitasking cannot inflate it further.
